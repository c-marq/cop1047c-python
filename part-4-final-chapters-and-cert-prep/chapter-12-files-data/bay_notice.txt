Welcome to Biscayne Bay, where shallow water, sea grass and sunshine make for a calm morning on the water. Please check in at the dock before you launch, and wear your life jacket at all times.

Manatees often rest in the warm shallows near the mangroves. Stay at least fifty feet away, never touch or feed them, and let them swim off on their own.

When the afternoon storms roll in, head back to the dock right away. The bay’s weather can change fast, and the staff will help every paddler get home safely.

# Getting Started: COP1047C Intro to Python

This guide gets you ready to use the practice pages. Setup takes about 5 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Get a Google Account

You write and run your code in **Google Colab**, which needs a Google account.

* If you already have a Gmail or Google account, you are ready
* If not, create a free account at [accounts.google.com](https://accounts.google.com)

> **Recommendation:** use your MDC student email or a personal Gmail. Either works. Just use the same account every time.

---

## Step 2: Test Google Colab

1. Go to [colab.research.google.com](https://colab.research.google.com)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click **New Notebook**
4. In the first cell, type this and press **Shift + Enter** to run it:

```python
print("Python is ready to go!")
```

If you see `Python is ready to go!` under the cell, you are set up correctly.

---

## Step 3: Use a Practice Page

1. Open the site: [c-marq.github.io/cop1047c-python](https://c-marq.github.io/cop1047c-python/)
2. Pick a page and keep it open next to your Colab tab
3. For each exercise:
    * Read the story and answer the **Think first** questions in your head
    * Copy the **plan** into a Colab cell. The plan is a list of comments: your to-do list
    * Write your code under `# YOUR CODE HERE`, one plan line at a time
    * Run it and compare with the **target run**
    * Stuck? Open **Hint 1**, then Hint 2, then Hint 3
    * Open the **solution** only after you have tried
4. Tick the checkbox when you finish an exercise, and rate each section 1 to 3

> **Important:** your checkmarks and ratings save in the browser you are using. They don't carry over to another device, another browser or a private window.

---

## Step 4: Save Your Work

Colab notebooks you create live in your Google Drive under **Colab Notebooks**.

1. Click the notebook name at the top of Colab and rename it, for example `Chapter 6 Practice`
2. Use one notebook per practice page so your work stays together
3. Colab saves as you go. Check the top of the page for "All changes saved"
